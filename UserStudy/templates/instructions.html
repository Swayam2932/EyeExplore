Study instructions

You will complete four tasks in EyeExplore. Follow the steps shown with each task and answer from the dashboard data.

{{ cohort|title }} participant · Set {{ set_number }}
  1. Complete the four tasks in order. Read every numbered step and inspect only the participant records and dashboard views named in that task.
  2. For each task, choose exactly one answer: A, B, C, or D. Choose D only if a required record, view, or data field is unavailable.
  3. After submitting an answer, rate each visualization used in that task from 1 (Very difficult to use) to 10 (Extremely easy to use). This is a separate step from answering.
  4. Your task time is measured from when the question appears until you submit your answer. The visualization rating is not included in that task time.
  5. Do not change the dataset, stimulus, participant selection, AOI, playback interval, or other study settings. Use the fixed values and steps provided.
  6. If a named view, record, stimulus, or required information is missing or fails to load, stop and tell the study leader. Do not guess or substitute another item.
  7. Your answer and visualization ratings are saved as you go. If the page reloads, continue with the same participant ID and access code.
  8. After the four tasks, you may optionally leave a written review on a separate final page.
  9. Wait for the saved confirmation before closing this page.
